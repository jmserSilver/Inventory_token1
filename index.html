Load data

Load a CSV with one row per item and warehouse. The file is read in your browser and nothing is uploaded or saved. Required columns:

sku, name, category, warehouse, unit_cost, unit_price, on_hand, reorder_point, avg_daily_demand, on_order, inbound_eta, age_days

Optional: fill_rate (0–1) and monthly history columns rec_jan … rec_dec, iss_jan … iss_dec, wo_jan … wo_dec (units), wo_reason. Without history, the Movements cards stay empty.
